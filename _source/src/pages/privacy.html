Privacy policy

This policy explains what we collect through this website, why we collect it, who we share it with and how you can have it deleted.

Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

Car Advisor Corporation, 1500 NW 89th Ct, Suite 112, Doral, FL 33172. You can reach us at info@caradvisorcorp.com or +1 (305) 600-6112.

What we collect

Only what you type into our contact form: your name, phone number, email address, the vehicle or service you are interested in, your budget range and any message you write. We do not ask for your Social Security number, your date of birth or your bank details on this website.

Why we collect it

To answer your request, to source and negotiate the vehicle you asked about, and to keep in touch with you about that request. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only with the parties needed to complete your request: the dealerships and lenders we approach on your behalf, and the transport company that delivers your vehicle. We share the minimum required, and only after you have asked us to move forward.

WhatsApp and phone

When you finish the form you can send us your request over WhatsApp. That message travels through WhatsApp and is subject to their own privacy policy as well as ours. Calls and text messages are handled by our phone provider.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your language and light or dark theme preference are stored in your own browser and never reach us.

How long we keep it

We keep your request for as long as we are working on it, and afterwards only as long as required for our accounting and legal obligations.

Your rights

Write to info@caradvisorcorp.com and we will tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Florida residents may also ask us to stop contacting them at any time.

Children

This website is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
