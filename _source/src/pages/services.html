What we do

Six services, one point of contact.

  • Custom vehicle sourcing

    Tell us the make, model and trim, or just the budget. We search our nationwide network for any vehicle, including EVs and hard-to-find trims, and evaluate each option before you commit.

  • Expert price negotiation

    We negotiate directly with dealers on your behalf for the lowest price and the best package. You skip the back-and-forth; we bring you the final number.

  • Financing & lease optimization

    We compare loan and lease rates across our network to secure the best terms, so a marked-up interest rate never reaches your contract.

  • Trade-in valuation

    We manage the valuation of your current car so you get top dollar for it, as part of the same stress-free process.

  • Contract & paperwork audit

    Before you sign, our experts review every line of your lease or purchase agreement and remove extras like nitrogen-filled tires, forced VIN etching or arbitrary market adjustments.

  • VIP doorstep delivery

    Once the paperwork is final, your vehicle is inspected, cleaned and delivered to your home or office. You only have to receive the keys.
